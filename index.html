MY NATIONAL JOURNEY ✈️

From my first cultural fest to representing my school at the National Level in Odisha — this is the journey that I will always carry in my heart.

🎨 The Beginning — 2024

In 2024, I participated in the Cultural Fest for the first time in the 2-D Painting category. It was the beginning of a journey that I never imagined would take me all the way to the National Level.

🏆 Cluster Level — 1st Place

My first competition was held at Ajmer Saundane. I gave my best and was extremely happy to secure 1st place at the Cluster Level.

That victory gave me the confidence to dream bigger.

🥇 State Level — 1st Place

The State Level competition was held at our own school, EMRS Pimpri. I participated in 2-D Painting once again and achieved 1st place.

When I went onto the stage, the students were cheering, shouting and whistling loudly. That moment is still one of my most unforgettable memories.

🇮🇳 Selected for Nationals

Then came the moment that changed everything.

Ojaswini Ma'am told me that the National Level competition would be held in Odisha and that our journey would be by flight.

Hearing that news made me incredibly excited. It was a feeling that was almost impossible to express in words.

From our school, only two students were selected for the National Level — me and Nivedita.

✈️ My First Flight

When the day finally arrived, we travelled by an AC bus to Mumbai's Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport.

After the checking and security procedures, we entered the airport and finally boarded the plane.

Nivedita and I were sitting together. When the plane started taking off, I felt an incredible mixture of happiness, excitement and nervousness. It was my first flight and a completely new experience for me.

🌴 Welcome to Odisha

After landing at Bhubaneswar Airport, we collected our luggage and waited for our transport. Soon, a large AC bus arrived to take us to our hotel.

🏨 Ten Days in Odisha

We stayed in the same hotel for around ten days. Our room had an AC, TV, refrigerator, washroom, balcony, cupboard and a comfortable sofa.

🛕 Exploring Odisha

On the first day, we were taken to some of the famous places of Odisha. We got the opportunity to experience the culture, history and beauty of the state.

Visiting places like the Jagannath Temple and other famous locations made the journey even more special.

🎨 The National Competition

From the second day onwards, our actual competition began. The competition was held at a university.

Every day, a bus came to pick us up from the hotel and take us to the university. We continued our competition and activities for several days.

I was representing my school in the 2-D Painting category at the National Level.

📸 Memories That Stay Forever

Beyond the competition, I made memories that I will never forget. Meeting students from different places, travelling together, exploring Odisha and experiencing everything for the first time made those ten days truly unforgettable.

🏅 The Final Result

Finally, after days of competition, the last day arrived — the day of the prize distribution.

Nivedita secured 1st place in Mimicry. I secured 2nd place in 2-D Painting at the National Level.

Standing there with a National Level achievement was an incredibly proud and emotional moment for me.

🎥 A Memory in Motion

Some moments are better experienced than explained. Here is one of the videos from my National Level journey.

✨ More Than Just a Competition

This journey taught me that a small beginning can lead to something much bigger than we imagine.

From my first Cultural Fest, to Cluster Level, then State Level, and finally the National Level in Odisha — every step gave me confidence, memories and a reason to keep creating.

This wasn't just a competition. It was a chapter of my life that I will remember forever. ❤️
